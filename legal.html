Service delivery, security and logs

The site is delivered by Netlify, Inc., San Francisco, USA, through its content delivery network. When you open a page, Netlify's servers receive the technical data every web request carries: IP address, request URL, date and time, browser, device and operating-system information, and the referring page. Netlify keeps these logs for a short time for stability, security and abuse prevention, and provides them to us only in aggregated form. This is necessary to show you the site at all.

Fonts

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA; for users in Switzerland and the EEA: Google Ireland Ltd.). To fetch a font file your browser sends its IP address and browser details to Google. Google states that it does not use Google Fonts requests for profiling or advertising and does not set cookies with them.

Game progress, scores and the daily comparison

When you finish a game, the page stores one row in a database run by Supabase, Inc. on servers in Ireland (EU, Amazon Web Services region eu-west-1):

day · game · score · maximum · device id

The device id is a random number your browser generates the first time you finish a game and keeps in its local storage. It is not derived from your device, your IP address or anything about you, and we cannot link it to a person. Its only purpose is to allow one result per device, game and day. The rows are used to show you how your score compares with everyone else's that day ("You beat 73 %"), and in aggregated form to see which puzzles are played and how hard they are. Nobody can read individual rows through the website; the page only receives counts per score. Supabase's servers see your IP address while the row is sent, as any server does, and keep short-term access logs.

Your current device id: . Quote it if you want your rows deleted.

Cookies, local storage and consent

Necessary storage (no consent needed): today's progress in each game, your past scores and streak, the device id, settings such as the last view, notices already shown, and your consent choice itself. This lives in your browser's local storage, never leaves your device except as described above, and stays until you clear the site data in your browser. The site then starts fresh, and old results in the database can no longer be attributed to your browser.

Optional storage: none at present. The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and shows no ads. Should that change, this policy will name the partners, and a consent banner will ask you before any advertising or analytics storage is set.

Optional storage (only with your consent, or where the law allows it without): consent, analytics and advertising cookies and identifiers, as described in the next section.

Retention and deletion

Server logs are kept for a short time by the hosting providers. Score rows are anonymous statistics and are kept without time limit; on request we delete the rows of a device id. Data in your browser stays until you delete it. Cookies and identifiers of advertising and analytics partners are kept for the periods shown in the consent banner, and are removed when you withdraw consent. Statutory retention duties and legitimate evidence needs, for example legal defence, remain unaffected.
