Hosting (server logs)

The site is delivered by Netlify, Inc., San Francisco, USA, through its content delivery network. When you open a page, Netlify's servers receive the technical data every web request carries: your IP address, the page requested, date and time, browser type and referring page. Netlify keeps these logs for a short time for security and to run the service, and provides them to us only in aggregated form. This is necessary to show you the site at all (Art. 31 para. 2 FADP). Netlify processes the data in the USA under its data processing agreement, which contains the EU standard contractual clauses recognised by the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner.

Fonts

The typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA; for users in Switzerland and the EEA: Google Ireland Ltd.). To fetch a font file your browser sends its IP address and browser details to Google. Google states that it does not use Google Fonts requests for profiling or advertising and does not set cookies with them. No other Google service is used.

Scores and the daily comparison

When you finish a game, the page stores one row in a database run by Supabase, Inc. on servers in Ireland (EU, Amazon Web Services region eu-west-1):

day · game · score · maximum · device id

The device id is a random number your browser generates the first time you finish a game and keeps in its local storage. It is not derived from your device, your IP address or anything about you, and we cannot link it to a person. Its only purpose is to allow one result per device, game and day. The rows are used to show you how your score compares with everyone else's that day ("You beat 73 %"). Nobody can read individual rows through the website; the page only receives counts per score. The rows are anonymous statistics and are kept without time limit. Supabase's servers see your IP address while the row is sent, as any server does, and keep short-term access logs.

Data in your browser (local storage)

The site keeps in your browser's local storage: today's progress in each game, your past scores and streak, the device id, and small settings such as the last view. This data never leaves your device except as described above. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser; the site then starts fresh, and old results in the database can no longer be attributed to your browser.

Sharing a score

"Share my score" hands a short text to your phone's share sheet or copies it to your clipboard. Nothing is sent to us or to a social network by the site itself.

Ringer (party game)

Ringer runs entirely on the phone it is played on. Names entered for a round are not stored and not transmitted.
